# Operator session: participant brief

Facilitators: give this page to each operator who takes part in an [operator usability check](../operator-usability-check.md). Everything below is addressed to the operator.

---

Thank you for taking part. We are testing the review screen, not you.

## What to expect

- Use your assigned test account and the way you normally work with a computer (mouse, keyboard only, screen reader or other assistive technology).
- Every record in this session is made up. Nothing you do affects a real customer.
- Work as you would on an ordinary shift.
- You may stop a task at any time.
- You may ask for help. The facilitator will note that you asked.

## The tasks

The facilitator gives you a card with your team, environment and target, and then one task at a time. After each task, tell the facilitator what you believe happened and what on the screen supports that.

1. A customer confirmed that the issue is fixed. Review the proposed ticket change and decide what to do.
2. The same customer needs a second review. Check the new proposal against your card and decide what to do.
3. The viewer asks you to sign in again while you are investigating an inventory. Continue, and explain what happened to the inventory.
4. A colleague asks whether an agent can access the cloud resource on the screen. Look at the relationship shown and report what you can establish.
5. A ticket update was submitted but its response was lost. Decide what to do next, and report the outcome you can verify.

The facilitator may give you the tasks in a different order.

## One rule

Do not put personal information, passwords or real customer text into anything in this session.
