# Operator session

Use your assigned test account and your usual access method. All records in this session are synthetic. Work as you would during an ordinary shift. You may stop a task at any time. After each task, tell the facilitator what you believe happened and what evidence supports that answer. The facilitator may record that you requested help.

The facilitator supplies the assigned team, environment and target on a separate task card. Complete one task at a time in the order provided.

- A customer confirmed the issue is fixed. Review the proposed ticket change and decide what to do.
- The same customer needs a second review. Inspect the new proposal against your assignment and decide what to do.
- Continue an inventory investigation after the viewer asks you to sign in again. Explain what happened to the inventory.
- A colleague wants to know whether an agent can access the displayed cloud resource. Inspect the relationship and report what you can establish.
- A ticket update lost its response after submission. Decide what to do next and report the outcome you can verify.

Do not put personal information, credentials or customer text in the study materials.
