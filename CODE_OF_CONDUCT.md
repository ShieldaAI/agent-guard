# Code of conduct

We want this project to be a place where people can work together without being
harassed, belittled or excluded.

## What we expect

- Be respectful. Disagree about ideas, not about people.
- Assume good intent, and say plainly when something is a problem.
- Give and accept feedback on the work. Keep it specific and kind.
- Do not share other people's private information.

## What is not acceptable

Harassment, insults, sexualized language or attention, threats, and discrimination of
any kind, in issues, pull requests, discussions or any other project space.

## Reporting

If someone's behavior breaks these rules, contact the maintainers through a
[private report](https://github.com/ShieldaAI/agent-guard/security/advisories/new) marked
"conduct", or through the contact details on the ShieldaAI organization page. Reports are
kept confidential. Maintainers may remove comments, close threads or block accounts.

This text follows the spirit of the
[Contributor Covenant](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/).
